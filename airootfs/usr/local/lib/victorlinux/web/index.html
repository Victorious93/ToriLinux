Choose a destination

Drives on this PC

Internal disks, external drives, USB sticks and SD cards. App state (settings, chats, API-key hash) needs a Linux filesystem (ext4/btrfs/xfs); exFAT, NTFS and FAT32 drives are fine for models, documents, downloads and backups. FAT32 cannot hold any single file of 4 GiB or more. A drive labelled VICTORDATA (legacy TORIDATA) is found automatically; nothing else needs a special label. The operating system itself does not persist.
